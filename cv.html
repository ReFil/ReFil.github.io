Research Assistant for University of Southampton (October 2020 to date)

I have assisted in numerous different research projects at the University of Southampton, primarily an IoT project with a commercial client, as well as leading the electrical installation of a PV test site at Chilbolton observatory.

The IoT project entails a significant amount of experimentation to evaluate the feasibility of different power sources for an IoT device, AirFuel-based wireless charging as well as PV power was considered and evaluated. Included in this was the development of our own AirFuel receiver.

The laboratory based at Chilbolton is still in progress, it consists of numerous different types of PV panels on different mountings, a suite of sensors including pyranometers and reference cells, and a custom datalogging setup I designed and built that tracks and logs the maximum power available from each individual PV panel in real time. Utility constraints at the site prevented us from using a conventional grid connection, so the energy harvested from the panels is used to power the datalogging system, the sensors deployed at the site, and excess energy is burned off in a heater.

This has expanded my proficiency in a wide range of topics and also given me experience teaching and managing a team of other demonstrators.
