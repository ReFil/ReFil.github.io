Low Level out on the Smart Roadster Grundig Head unit

DATE

The Smart Roadster's sound system benefits massively from an additional subwoofer, and there's a great spot behind the seats to chuck it too. Most external car subwoofers can take "Low Level" audio input through RCA jacks, which is essentially the audio signal prior to amplification, or "High Level", which is the post amplified signal sent to the main speakers, but some are low level only. A lot of people will tap into the speaker wiring for a high level signal, then add some attenuator box to get the low level signal if needed but that's a bit janky for me. Other people would ditch the stock head unit in favour of a gneric single din one and a framce but the stock one is very well sculpted to fit the dash and looks quite good, so I wanted to see if one could get the low level signals out the stock stereo

ISO Stereo connection standard

Back in the good old days of the 2000s there was a semi common standard for car stereo connections that a lot of manufacturers abided by before cars started getting full RTOS based computers to play baby shark at full volume out the speakers (looking at you VW with your weird QNX MIB boxes). This standard is still used by most aftermarkert stereo manufactuers and is known as the ISO 10487 standard (below picture from pinoutguide.com / pinouts.ru).

The Smart Grundig head unit

The stock head unit in a Smart Roadster is actually pretty decent, it hasa built in audio crossover so you can hook tweeters up, and sounds pretty decent too. Like many radios from the early 2000s it has a built in pin system to deter theft, with all the electrical gremlins I've had with the car necessitating battery removal I've been able to commit the pin to memory. It uses one part of the ISO C connector as a line in, to which i have a bluetooth audio adapter connected. It was a right pain in the arse finding an adapter that supports an actually good audio codec, and i dont think the AptX module i found is still available, but I do recommend trying to hunt down and AptX one if you can as you can tell the difference.
