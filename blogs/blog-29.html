FIDO2 USB HID to NFC hardware proxy

DATE

Java Cards are really cool, and I guarantee you, the reader, have at least one, probably more in your posession. Bank cards, SIM cards, ePassports are all secretly microcontrollers running Java VMs, and the only difference is in the contact specification, (Dual Contact+NFC, Contact only or RFID only) and deployed applications (referred to as applets). Given their mass proliferation they've been fairly well developed and are very secure environments, and can take on the role of a security token in many contexts. .
