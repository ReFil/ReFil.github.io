Pinecil soldering iron

As I'm sure wont come as a surprise to a lot of people my soldering iron is a pinecil, I used to use a TS100 which i'd bodged a type C PD trigger board into for PD operation but i use my iron a lot and the TS100 started falling apart after a couple years. My primary tip is the conical high power tip, with the sharp J shaped tip for the fine work.
