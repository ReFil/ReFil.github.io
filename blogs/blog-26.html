I'm so tired of the slop era

DATE

T his is not exactly my usual sort of project blog post but I don't use mainstream social media and have wanted to write down my thoughts on this for a while. LLMs, "AI", slop machines, codex, Claude, ChatGPT or whatever the latest one is this week (last i heard there was something called Jev??) are the latest (and I use this term extremely charitably) "innovation" the big tech world has delivered unto us peons, alongside this months barrage of catastrophic data breaches, zero days and Windows updates that manage to continually decrease the functionality of my work laptop. Discussion on its use is polarised, with a large group of people fundamentally opposed to their usage, and another group completely enamoured with the technology, it's current uses and it's potential.

I do not like this technology, the way it is marketed nor the way it is used. The benefits cannot outweight the massive harms caused to the world by these systems. My interaction with these systems is primarilyt through a technical lens, after all I work in a technical field, but it's not possible to coldly view these systems as code completion engines, intellisense but for ideas. The same LLM that's ruining your codebase is sending someone else into a psychosis episode that will end with them ending their life. Engineering and programming are creative fields, and

Part of me wonders why bother listing the pros and cons of generative AI usage like it's going to change anyone's mind, but I feel like it should be done regardless for completeness' sake if nothing else and to display the mass disparity beteen the benefits and disadvantages (some of which are insurmountable) of this technology.

The bad things pr2: sociological

These systems are enabled by a degree of industrial theft to an extent never before seen in human history. Every sort of work imaginable from code, fiction and non-fiction works of literature, scientific articles, news, blogs, social media posts is being fed into giant industrial information shredders and reconstitutors to be regurcitated back to you for the low low fee of just $10 a month for slop lite or just $200 a month for slop pro.

The bad things pt3: environmental

Why I care and why you should care

Generative AI tools have made being on the internet a less enjoyable and pleasant experience. Ignoring the technofacist horrors they're enabling on a massive scale they have ruined the fun I used to have looking through Hackaday articles and browing new developments on GitHub and suchlike. Nowadays it's a crapshoot whether the project will be some delicately crafted masterpiece clearly born from the creators dedication and passion for the art of programming or is it unnecessarily long and unnecessarily complex. There are some isntances whhen I can look past egregiously vibe coded actual code, but when the associated project documentation, README, website and even blogpost are all clearly generated then whats actually the achievement here. If the creator didn't even have the passion in what they "made" to write it up in their own words then why should I assume they had the passion to check it actually works properly. I've seen many instances of vibe projects that have a very impressve suite of CI tests and yet they still miss egregious bugs that were obvious to anyone actually using the project, so all the big CI suite achieved was wasting massive amounts of GitHub actions time and instilling a false sense of overconfidence. I also fear for the deskilling of a generation of engineers and programmers.

They have also made some of my work more frustrating and less fun. As a maintainer of public facing open source projects it is inevitable that LLM generated code, issues and PRs would rock up and whilst sometimes the work makes sense and the submitter is respectful of the knowledge gap between the maintainer and someone prompting Claude other times they are not and will generate an unreasonably large code change based on some very vauge prompt like "Fix this problem", get the LLM to summarise these changes in an unnecessarily verbose and patronising manner, dump that pulled hand grenade in my lap and expect me to untangle the mess. To put it bluntly I do not care about the technical merits of some subset of a large patch when it is delivered to me without respect and understanding for the time and knowledge it takes to review a PR in a more in depth manner than "LGTM :thumbsup:".
