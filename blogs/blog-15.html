Prepping a car for the Twing Raid Part 1: Prepping and Electrical

DATE

The Twing Raid is an event where you and 249-299 other teams grab a first generation Renault Twingo and send it from San Sebastien in Spain all the way down into Morocco, around the Moroccan desert and back to Spain over the course of two weeks. It looks like an incredible adventure. I first learnt about it in an article in The Drive, then I sent the srticle to my best friend, who responded with "We should do that", and in my infinite wisdom I decided to go onto the Twing Raid website and book tickets for the 2026 running there and then. This was despite not having any sort of plan, not speaking a word of french (it's run by a french travel agency and seems to be a primarily french event) and definitely not having a twingo, which wasn't even sold in the UK originally.

We're slowly preparing the car for the run in Febuary next year, there's a lot to do, including a lot of administrative tasks as well as the more fun task of building the car. There's a lot to learn (including french!) and a long list of tasks, but just as important as doing the work itself is how to approach a project as massive as this, what systems to use, how to decide what to do when, and when important deadlines are. It's pointelss havving the Rolls-Royce of offroading twingos if we're 2 weeks late to the start line

Preparation and Organisation
