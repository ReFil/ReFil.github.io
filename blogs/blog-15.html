DVLA MOT Checker Bot

DATE

My friends and I have a long and colourful history with various automotive shitboxes, and often we want to follow their journeys after they depart our custody. Usually this takes the form of putting it through the GOV.UK MOT checker website whenever we remember but I thought it would be more fun to have a little bot that sends out an email when a car on our list has its mot about to expire, when it has an MOT and with the information on whether it failed or passed (and how badly it failed, if it failed).

It turns out that the DVLA MOT checker has an accessible API, you just have to fill out a form to request access to it, it's not even paid or anything, so I started looking more seriously into this. I've also wanted to do some proper projects in Rust, so this seemed like as good a project as any to properly learn rust on.
