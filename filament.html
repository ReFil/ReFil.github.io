S CF PLA Pros: High detail, low warp, matte finish

Cons: Slightly more expensive 		High-Speed PLA Pros: Very fast printing
Cons: Low heat resistance 		CF PETG Pros: Strong, stable, good finish
Cons: Needs hardened nozzle 		PCTG Pros: Tough, easy, low warp, strong layers
Cons: Expensive, hard to find 		Glass-Filled Nylon Pros: Extremely strong, heat resistant, less warp
Cons: Abrasive, needs advanced setup 		TPU (mid hardness) Pros: Flexible, impact-proof, durable
Cons: Needs dry filament, sticky
A PLA Pros: Cheap, easy, great quality
Cons: Weak to heat, UV, brittle 		PETG Pros: Tough, heat resistant
Cons: Stringing, poor bridging 		ASA Pros: UV/weather resistant, strong
Cons: Warping, fumes 		PC-CF Pros: Extremely stiff
Cons: Hard to print 		SEBS Pros: Stretchy, smooth prints
Cons: Moisture sensitive, tuning needed
B Silk PLA Pros: Shiny, aesthetic
Cons: Weak, clogging risk 		Wood PLA Pros: Realistic texture
Cons: Weak, moisture sensitive 		Matte PLA Pros: Nice finish, sandable
Cons: Weaker, absorbs moisture 		Nylon (PA6/PA12) Pros: Strong, durable
Cons: Warping, moisture issues 		Polycarbonate Pros: Very strong, heat resistant
Cons: Hard to print, slow
C PMMA (Acrylic) Pros: Clear, UV resistant
Cons: Brittle, warps 		Low-soft TPU Pros: Very flexible
Cons: Hard to print, impractical 		Nylon Blends Pros: Mixed properties
Cons: Combined weaknesses

Cons: Weak, expensive 		Polypropylene (PP) Pros: Tough in theory
Cons: Terrible adhesion, warping 		HDPE Pros: None
Cons: Extreme warping, unusable 		POM (Acetal) Pros: Low friction
Cons: No adhesion, toxic risk
